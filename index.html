About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Nishida Laboratory at Kyushu Institute of Technology, within the Graduate School of Life Science and Systems Engineering.

My research focuses on underwater robotics and navigation for autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs). I am particularly interested in multi-IMU sensor fusion, visual odometry, and fault-tolerant navigation.

My recent work investigates a distributed multi-IMU framework with rigid-body kinematic constraints to mitigate short-to-mid-term navigation drift.

I also write on my personal blog ↗.

Research Interests

My interests center on reliable navigation and state estimation for underwater robots.

01

AUV Navigation

Navigation and state estimation for autonomous underwater vehicles, with a focus on inertial drift and multi-IMU sensor fusion.

02

Visual Odometry

Visual motion estimation and its role in underwater robot navigation.

03

Fault-Tolerant Navigation

Reliable navigation through sensor redundancy and multi-sensor fusion.
